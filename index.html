CALCULATORS / GEAR

Gear crafting

Item recipes and crafting return rates imported from your updated workbook.

ⓘ How to Use This Calculator
  1. Select your item. Search by name or expand the category dropdowns, then choose tier, enchantment, and quantity.
  2. Set up your craft. Select the crafting location, daily bonus, and whether you used focus. Total focus spent is optional.
  3. Check resource return rates. Review the automatic rates, or expand Advanced to enter a custom return rate.
  4. Enter material prices. Fetch available Americas market prices or type prices manually. Check in-game prices before crafting.
  5. Review your results. Check materials required, total crafting cost, cost per item, and estimated profit. Expand Fees to adjust station fees or sales tax.
  6. Save your batch. Optionally enter a selling price and select Save craft to My Journal to track the batch and future sales.

Market prices and profit figures are estimates. Confirm actual market prices and crafting costs in-game before making a decision.

CALCULATORS / RESOURCES

Refining

Estimate refining cost and net profit from your workbook's tier quantities.

ⓘ How to Use This Calculator
  1. Choose a resource. Select cloth, leather, metal bars, planks, or stone blocks, then choose the tier and enchantment.
  2. Enter available materials. Set the amount of raw resources and, where required, previous-tier refined materials. You can adjust the autofilled quantity.
  3. Enter prices and fees. Supply raw-resource prices, previous-tier material costs, expected selling price per refined unit, and station fee.
  4. Set return bonuses. Choose whether you used focus, whether you are refining in the bonus city, and any daily refining bonus. Advanced lets you override the return rate.
  5. Review the estimate. Check expected refined output, effective cost per unit, and estimated net profit.
  6. Save the batch. Use Save refining batch to My Journal to track the production and record sales later.

Refining returns are estimates; actual quantities and market prices can vary. Verify costs and bonuses in-game.

TRACKING / PROFIT

My Journal

Track crafting batches, partial sales, and actual profit. Saved only in this browser.

ⓘ How to Use My Journal
  1. Add a batch. Save a craft from Gear Crafting or Refining, or use Add a production batch to enter it manually.
  2. Review your overview. Expand crafting-class, item, and tier folders to see individual batches and their costs, quantities, and profits.
  3. Track unsold items. Open Active Inventory to see remaining quantities and their associated production costs.
  4. Record a sale. Choose the batch you sold from, enter the quantity and sale details, and save the transaction. Partial sales leave the unsold balance in inventory.
  5. Review your history. Use Transaction History and the available filters to examine recorded sales and realized profit.
  6. Correct or protect records. Delete a test batch from its individual entry if needed, and regularly export a JSON backup. Import a backup to restore records.

Journal records are saved in this browser, not shared across devices. Back up your data before clearing browser storage or switching devices.

Add a production batch

Use the Save to Journal buttons in Crafting or Refining to copy estimated costs, or enter a batch manually here. Cost is total for the entire batch, including station fees and expected returns.

For focus batches, actual focus can be entered manually or copied automatically from Gear Crafting. Without-focus cost is required to calculate the additional value created by focus.

Record a sale

Profit = sale proceeds after fees − production cost allocated to sold items. Profit % (on cost) = realized profit ÷ allocated crafting cost × 100. For example, 100 silver cost and 150 silver net proceeds = 50%. This differs from conventional net profit margin (profit ÷ net sales), which is also shown above. Unsold items remain inventory.
